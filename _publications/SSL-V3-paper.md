**Keywords:** 

Contrastive learning, Data imbalance, NR-VQA, Self-supervised learning, Video classification, ViViT

**Contributions:**

- We propose the Combined-SSL framework, which is a theoretical contribution that leverages the reciprocal relationship between the VQA task and Contrastive Learning to classify videos objectively.
- We develop the SSL-V3 model to implement the Combined-SSL mechanism.
- We present a hierarchical VQA head designed to regress video quality score, incorporating Sequence Score Regressor and Video Score Regressor modules.
- We design a new Loss function to address class imbalance issues and control training at batch and subject levels.

**Production:** SSL-V3, Combined-SSL mechanism, hierarchical VQA head, new loss.
